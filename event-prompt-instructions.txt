EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: COMPLETE EVENT & TICKET ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
1. **Event Types & Formats**:
   - `category`: 'fundraiser' | 'pitch-night' | 'workshop' | 'gala'
   - `type`: 'physical' | 'virtual' | 'hybrid'
   - `pricingType`: 'free' | 'paid'
   - `remainingSeats`: Dynamically computed as `Math.max(0, capacity - reservedCount)`
   - Speakers subdocuments (`name`, `role`, `avatar`), `dressCode`, `virtualLink`, `featured`, `status` ('draft' | 'published' | 'cancelled' | 'completed').

2. **Automated Registration Workflow (JWT Authenticated)**:
   - **User Session Authentication**: Users must be authenticated (`Authorization: Bearer <token>`). Attendee Name and Email are securely retrieved by the backend directly from the authenticated user profile—NEVER passed in the request body.
   - **Virtual Events**: Always FREE. Immediately confirmed. Confirmation email dispatches the private virtual meeting link (`virtualLink`). No physical ticket is generated.
   - **Physical & Hybrid Events (FREE)**: Free RSVP. Immediately confirmed. Auto-generates unique Ticket ID (`IFA-YYYY-XXXXXX`) and scannable QR code. Confirmation email sent to attendee and admin (`config.support.admin`).
   - **Physical & Hybrid Events (PAID)**: Creates pending booking. Redirects user to Stripe Checkout (`POST /api/v1/booking`). Webhook catches `checkout.session.completed`, confirms booking, generates Ticket ID + QR code, creates `Transaction` (`category: 'Event'`, `type: 'Credit'`), and emails golden ticket to attendee and admin.

3. **Door Staff Live Check-in Scanner**:
   - `POST /api/v1/booking/check-in/:ticketCode` (or `POST /api/v1/booking/check-in` with `{ ticketCode }`).
   - The QR code on tickets and emails encodes the clean `ticketCode` (e.g. `IFA-2026-B28A2B`), but the endpoint is smart and also accepts full ticket URLs or MongoDB `_id`s.
   - Verifies ticket authenticity, flags unpaid/cancelled bookings, prevents duplicate admissions by returning the exact previous check-in timestamp.

4. **Luxury Gold & Obsidian Black Printable Ticket Template**:
   - Modeled after the official golden invitation pass with the official iFundAyiti logo.
   - Perforated tear-stub design with Haiti map watermark, hibiscus botanical motif, scannable QR code, barcode, admit count, and `@media print` PDF download support.
   - Render URL: `GET /api/v1/booking/ticket/:idOrCode`.

- **Behavior**: If status is updated to `cancelled`, the backend automatically decrements `reservedCount` on the associated Event to free up seat capacity.
